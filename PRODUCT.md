# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are parents and caregivers in and around the San Gabriel Valley looking for an outing where young children can play while adults enjoy a real café experience. A second important audience is families evaluating the venue for a birthday or other private celebration.

## Product Purpose

My Little Paris helps families confidently choose and book a visit, meal, event, or celebration at a French café, indoor playground, and private event space. Success means visitors can picture their family there, understand the available experiences, and move naturally toward a reservation or party inquiry.

## Positioning

My Little Paris combines Montessori-inspired indoor play for young children with a full French café experience for adults, plus hosted events and private celebrations in the same family-centered venue.

## Operating Context

Visitors typically browse on the public marketing site before reserving a two-hour Eat & Play session, checking the food and drinks, discovering an event, or comparing party options. Photography is an important part of evaluating the atmosphere, space, food, and finished celebrations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public website is localized in English, French, and Chinese.
- Reservations and party inquiries are existing conversion paths and must remain distinct.
- The gallery is organized around four existing content categories: Events, Food, The Space, and Moments (the internal `misc` image folder).
- Gallery surfaces should support a mixed overview, category filtering, and a configurable preselected category for contextual reuse.
- Gallery photographs open into an accessible full-screen lightbox with previous and next navigation.
- The site is a responsive Next.js application and must retain semantic HTML, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, and performant image delivery.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is My Little Paris Café & Play. Existing brand assets, warm family voice, French identity, deep navy primary color, pink secondary color, and real venue photography should be preserved. The experience should feel polished and magical without becoming childish or making unsupported claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing site copy and business facts in `src/data`, `src/i18n`, and the current route files.
- Existing logo and established venue imagery in `public/images`.
- A larger real-photo library grouped under `public/gallery/events`, `public/gallery/food`, `public/gallery/misc`, and `public/gallery/the-space`.
- No new testimonials, attendance figures, or performance claims were provided and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Let real family, food, and venue imagery make the experience tangible.
- Keep play, dining, events, and celebrations connected as parts of one place.
- Make exploration inviting, then offer the right next action without pressuring the visitor.
- Preserve practical clarity beneath the warmth and personality.
- Build every public experience for all three supported languages and for mobile first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public experiences must be keyboard accessible, use meaningful alternative text, preserve visible focus, respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid relying on color alone to communicate filter state.
